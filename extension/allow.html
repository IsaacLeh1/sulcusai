Let SulcusAI read console and network logs?

SulcusAI reads the logs of its own tabs only (its tab group), to help find why a page breaks. Addresses and messages are read; cookies and sign-in headers are not.

While a SulcusAI tab is being read, the browser shows a bar saying SulcusAI "started debugging this browser". That is expected. Closing the bar or the tab stops it.
