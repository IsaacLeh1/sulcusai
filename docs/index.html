SulcusAI

A private AI workspace that runs on your own computer. It finds the AI models your PC can actually run, installs them with one click, and keeps everything local unless you choose otherwise.

Download for Windows, macOS or Linux

Chat and agents

Chat with local models, let the assistant work in folders you share, and undo its changes.

Mail and calendar

Connect Gmail, Outlook or any IMAP account and your calendars. Read, search and write mail with the assistant's help.

Voice and meetings

Dictation, voice chats, and meeting transcripts with speakers and action items, all on your PC.

Pictures, music and video

Make and edit images, music and short videos with models that run on your graphics card.

Private by design

  • No SulcusAI account, servers, analytics or tracking.
  • Chats, mail and calendars are encrypted on your computer.
  • Offline by default. Web access and online AI providers are switches you turn on yourself, and every request is logged.
  • Signing in with Google or Microsoft happens in your own browser; SulcusAI never sees your password.
