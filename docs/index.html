Private by design

Your work stays on your computer.

By default the AI runs on your own PC, and your chats, files, mail and calendars stay there.

Read the privacy policy

  • No account, no tracking

    No SulcusAI account, servers, analytics or tracking.

  • Encrypted on your PC

    Chats, mail and calendars are encrypted on your computer.

  • Offline by default

    Web access and online AI providers are switches you turn on yourself, and every request is logged.

  • Your password stays with you

    Signing in with Google or Microsoft happens in your own browser. SulcusAI never sees your password.

Open source

Free to use. Open to read.

SulcusAI's code is available under the GNU AGPL v3.0 on GitHub, so you can read the code that runs on your computer.
