# Consent for faces, voices and LoRAs

Version 1, 2026-11-03

SulcusAI can learn a voice from recordings, make talking pictures from a photo, and train LoRAs (small add-on models) on pictures of a face, a style or a voice. These are powerful, and they are about real people. These rules apply whenever you use them.

## The rule

**Only use a real person's face, voice or likeness with their consent.** Yours is fine. Anyone else's needs their clear permission for that use, before you start.

## What counts as consent

- **Clear:** they know what you will make (a voice that can say anything, a face model, a LoRA) and agreed to it.
- **For that use:** consent to a photo isn't consent to a face model; consent for a birthday video isn't consent for an advert.
- **From an adult:** a person under 18 can't give it alone; a parent or guardian must agree too. Never make sexual or suggestive content of anyone under 18, in any form.
- **Withdrawable:** if they change their mind, delete the voice, model or LoRA and what you made with it that you still control.
- **Kept:** keep a record (a message or note) of who agreed, to what, and when.

## Never, even with consent

- Sexual or intimate content of a real person who hasn't agreed to exactly that, or of anyone under 18.
- Making it look as if a real person said or did something to deceive people, damage their reputation, influence an election, or commit fraud (for example a cloned voice asking for money).
- Getting past a voice check, a bank's verification or any other security that relies on a voice or face.
- Copying a performer's voice or face to replace their paid work without a license.

## Public figures and the dead

Being famous isn't consent. Many places give people, and the families of people who have died, rights over their name, face and voice. Commentary and clearly labeled parody may be allowed where you live; check before you share.

## Sharing what you make

- Say it is AI-made when it could be taken as real.
- Don't share a voice model, face model or LoRA of a real person unless they agreed to it being shared.
- When you share a LoRA, include what it was trained on and its license.

## In the app

- The first time you turn on voice learning, talking pictures or LoRA training, the app shows a short notice with these rules and asks you to confirm.
- Each voice or face you add asks you to confirm it is yours or that you have consent.
- Everything stays on your PC unless you share it.

## Responsibility

These are your choices and your responsibility, as the Terms of Use say. If someone made something of you without consent, see "Reporting" in the Community Guidelines.
