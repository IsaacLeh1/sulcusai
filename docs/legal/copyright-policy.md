# SulcusAI Copyright Policy

Version 1, 2026-11-03

## What SulcusAI is for

SulcusAI shares **your own** files, models and creations with people you choose. It is not a way to get other people's paid content, and it must never be used or presented as one. Share someone else's work only when its license or its owner allows it.

## Who to contact about what

The publisher of SulcusAI hosts no one's content. Groups, shares, networks and relays run on people's own PCs, and the publisher can't see, remove or block anything in them.

| Where the material is | Who can act | How |
|---|---|---|
| In a group | The group's owners and moderators | Report inside the group, or contact them directly |
| In a share or on a relay or server | The person who runs it | Contact them; the "Running a relay or server" guide asks operators to publish a contact |
| On someone's own PC | That person | Contact them, or the courts or authorities where they are |
| On the SulcusAI website, in the app's own files, or in the catalog of models the app offers | The publisher | Send a notice to the contact below |

## Notices to the publisher

For the website, the software itself or the model catalog, send a notice to [copyright contact address] with:

1. your name and how to reach you;
2. the work you say is infringed;
3. exactly where the material is (a link, or the catalog entry);
4. a statement that you believe in good faith the use isn't authorized by the owner, its agent or the law;
5. a statement that the notice is accurate and, under penalty of perjury, that you are the owner or may act for the owner;
6. your physical or electronic signature.

The publisher removes or disables material that a valid notice identifies, and tells whoever supplied it where possible. If you think material was removed by mistake, you can send a counter-notice with the same details, a statement under penalty of perjury that it was removed by mistake, and consent to the courts named in the Terms of Use.

## Models and outputs

- Models come under their own licenses. Following them is up to the person who uses or shares the model.
- AI output can resemble existing work. Before publishing it, check that you have the rights to.
- LoRAs and fine-tunes made from someone else's work, face or voice need the rights or consent described in "Consent for faces, voices and LoRAs".

## Repeat infringers

Group owners and relay operators should remove people who infringe again and again. For the website and catalog, the publisher removes repeat infringers' entries.
