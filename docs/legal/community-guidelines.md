# SulcusAI Community Guidelines

Version 1, 2026-11-03

SulcusAI's groups, calls, shares and networks run on people's own PCs. Nobody watches them from above: the publisher of SulcusAI can't see, moderate or remove anything in them. That makes these guidelines a shared standard that each person keeps, and that each group's owners and moderators enforce in their own groups.

## Everyone

- **Be the reason people feel safe.** No harassment, threats, stalking, hate aimed at people for who they are, or encouraging anyone to hurt themselves.
- **Consent first.** Never share intimate images of anyone without their consent, including AI-made ones. Never copy a real person's face or voice, or put words in their mouth, without their consent. See "Consent for faces, voices and LoRAs".
- **No sexual content involving minors, ever.** This includes drawn and AI-made content. It is reported to the authorities.
- **No scams, spam or impersonation.** Don't pretend to be someone else, or to speak for someone you don't.
- **No malware or attacks.** Don't share harmful files, break into devices, or overload relays and networks.
- **Respect others' work.** Share your own files and models, and others' only when their license or owner allows it. See the Copyright Policy.
- **Label AI.** When something you share was made by AI and could be taken as real (a photo, a voice, a video of a real place or person), say so.
- **Follow the law where you are.**

## Group owners and moderators

You run your group on your own PC, and these guidelines are the floor, not the ceiling.

- Write your group's rules, and enforce them fairly.
- Act on reports from members: warn, mute, remove or ban.
- Remove anything illegal as soon as you know of it, and report crimes to the authorities (below).
- Mark groups with adult content 18+. Never let anyone under 18 into them.
- You are the operator of your group. The publisher has no access to it and can't act on reports about it.

## Age and teen settings

- **Minimum age:** 13. People under 13 may not use SulcusAI's social features.
- **13 to 17:** a parent or guardian must agree to the Terms of Use for them.
- **The age check:** social features ask for a date of birth when first turned on. It is kept on the device, and lying about it breaks these guidelines.
- **Teen settings** start on for anyone under 18, and stay on until they turn 18:
  - message requests from people they don't share a group with are held for review;
  - their profile and online status are shown only to people in their groups;
  - 18+ groups, uncensored models and market analytics are not available;
  - sensitive pictures from others are blurred until they choose to see them;
  - remote control of their PC is off.
- These settings are safeguards and can't be turned off by a person under 18.

## Reporting

**Inside a group:** use Report on a message or member. Reports go to that group's moderators, who run the group on their own PCs. You can also block or mute anyone at any time.

**Crimes go to the authorities, not to the publisher,** who can't see or act on anything in groups. The app links to the right place:

- **Someone in immediate danger:** call your local emergency number (911 in the US and Canada, 112 in the EU, 999 in the UK, 000 in Australia).
- **Child sexual abuse material or child exploitation:** in the US, the NCMEC CyberTipline (report.cybertip.org, 1-800-843-5678); elsewhere, your country's hotline listed by INHOPE (inhope.org).
- **Intimate images shared without consent:** StopNCII.org (helps block images on participating platforms), and in the US, Take It Down (takeitdown.ncmec.org) for images of people under 18.
- **Online fraud and other cybercrime:** in the US, the FBI's IC3 (ic3.gov); elsewhere, your national police or cybercrime unit.
- **Threats, stalking or harassment:** your local police.

## If someone breaks these guidelines

Group owners and moderators decide what happens in their groups. Anyone can block, mute and leave. Breaking the law can lead to a report to the authorities by the people affected.

## Changes

These guidelines may change. The version number and date at the top show which version you have, and the app asks you to read them again when a change matters.
