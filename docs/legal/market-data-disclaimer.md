# Market Data Disclaimer

Version 1, 2026-11-03

This applies when you turn on prices or market analytics in SulcusAI. The switch starts off, and it is for adults only.

- Prices, charts, summaries and analytics in SulcusAI are information, not advice.
- Nothing in SulcusAI, including anything an AI model says, is a recommendation or offer to buy, sell or hold any stock, fund, currency, cryptocurrency or other asset.
- SulcusAI never buys, sells or trades anything, and has nothing to do with money beyond showing prices.
- The publisher of SulcusAI is not a broker, dealer, investment adviser or financial planner, and receives nothing from any market data source or financial service.

## The data can be wrong

- Prices come from third-party sources you choose, under their own terms. They can be delayed, incomplete or wrong, and may stop at any time.
- AI models can misread numbers, invent figures or reason badly about markets. Check anything that matters against the original source.
- Past performance doesn't predict future results.

## Your decisions are yours

Any financial decision you make is yours alone, and so is the risk. For advice about your situation, talk to a licensed professional where you live.
